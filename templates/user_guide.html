Welcome to the User Guide

This guide explains how to use the site as a user.

Gallery

The gallery allows you to discover the available services:

  • Braids Gallery: Services related to braids.
    •
  • Treatment Gallery: Hair treatment services.
    •
  • Extensions Gallery: Hair extensions services.
    •

Registration

To create an account:

  1. Fill out the registration form with your information (username, email, password).
  2. An OTP code will be sent to your email address.
  3. Enter the OTP code on the confirmation page to activate your account.
  4. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder.

Login

To log in:

  1. Click on "Login" in the sidebar or the main menu.
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click on "Login".

Booking

To book a service:

  1. Navigate through the gallery to find a service that interests you.
  2. Click on "Book Appointment Now".
  3. Select a date and time for your appointment.
  4. Wait for the administrator's confirmation by email. If you don't see it quickly, check your spam folder.

Manage your reservations

To manage your reservations, modify, or delete:

  1. Click on "My Reservations" in the sidebar of the main menu.
  2. You will see a list of your reservations.
  3. Click on "Edit" to change the reservation details if it has not yet been validated or rejected.
  4. Click on "Delete" to cancel the reservation.
  5. You can also view the status of your reservation (validated, rejected, pending).
  6. If you have any questions, contact the administrator via the contact form.

Searching for Services

To search a service

  1. Go to our homepage and enter a keyword or phrase in the search bar.
  2. Press the "search" key or click the search button to initiate the search.
  3. Search results will be displayed, showing services that match your search criteria.
  4. You can browse through the search results to find the service that best suits your needs.

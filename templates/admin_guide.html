Welcome to the Administrator Guide

Dashboard

The dashboard displays global statistics:

  • Total number of reservations.
  • Total number of users.
  • Reservation breakdown by status (Validated, Denied, etc.).

Reservation Management

You can validate or reject pending reservations:

  1. Go to the "Reservations" section in the sidebar.
  2. Click on "Validate" or "Reject" for each reservation.
  3. A confirmation or rejection email is automatically sent to the client.

User Management

You can manage registered users:

  • Add, edit, or delete users.
  • Promote a standard user to administrator.
  • Demote an administrator to standard user.

Administrator Management

You can add new administrators:

  1. Go to the "Add Admin" section.

Searching for Services

To search a service

  1. Go to our homepage and enter a keyword or phrase in the search bar.
  2. Press the "search" key or click the search button to initiate the search.
  3. Search results will be displayed, showing services that match your search criteria.
  4. You can browse through the search results to find the service that best suits your needs.
